# Logo

## Anatomy

The DIGI-TEXX logo is the eight letters **D-I-G-I-T-E-X-X** with **6 square blue dots**. The dots stand for electronic circuits and the 6 services DIGI-TEXX provides. The tagline **"advanced digitizing services"** sits under the wordmark.

| Version | Build | File |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal (primary) | One small blue dot at top-left of "D", black wordmark "DIGI-TEXX", 5 stacked blue dots at right, ®, gray tagline below | `assets/logos/logo-horizontal.png` |
| Square / stacked (variant) | Navy rounded tile with radial gradient (`#1A489E` center → `#0D1120` edge), white "DIGI" over white rule over "TEXX", 5 blue dots column at right, ® | `assets/logos/logo-square.png` |

Construction grid from the guideline:
- Horizontal: margins 1X on all sides, wordmark width 8.8X, height 2X (X = 1 unit of the dot size).
- Square: margins 1X, tile width 4.9X, height 4X.

## Size and clear space

- Clear space: at least **½ of the logo-symbol height** on every side. Nothing (text, other logos, image edges) enters this zone. On print layouts the guideline shows this as ≥ 10 mm.
- Minimum size: **10 mm** wide in print. On screen use ≥ 120 px wide for horizontal, ≥ 48 px for square.
- With partner logos: align on a shared baseline/center line, keep equal visual weight, keep clear space between logos.

## Background rules

Preferred: white or light backgrounds (`#FFFFFF`, `#F0F0F0`).

| Background | Horizontal logo | Square logo |
|---|---|---|
| White / light gray | Full color | Full color tile |
| DIGI-TEXX Blue `#2582D7` | Mono white (`logo-horizontal-mono-white.png`) | White tile, blue letters (`logo-square-on-blue.png`) |
| Black / dark / navy | White wordmark + blue dots (`logo-horizontal-white.png`) | Full color tile (`logo-square.png`) or `logo-square-on-blue.png` |
| Mid gray | Full color on light gray; white on dark gray | Same |
| Photo / texture (metal, gold, wood) | Mono white or mono black — whichever has clear contrast | `logo-square-on-blue.png` (white tile) or `logo-square-mono-black.png` |
| Busy pattern | **Not allowed** — add a solid panel behind the logo or move it | — |

Single-color logos (white or black) are also used for B/W print and special materials (engraved metal, plastic, wood).

## Don'ts (all are forbidden)

1. Change element positions, change logo colors, or add elements.
2. Use a low-resolution, blurry logo.
3. Add shadow, glow, bevel, or any effect.
4. Place the logo on a busy patterned background.
5. Change the font of "DIGI-TEXX" (never re-type the wordmark — always use the asset).
6. Rotate the logo.
7. Squash or stretch the logo (always scale proportionally).
8. Make an outline/line version.

Sub-brand logos follow the same rules as the main logo (no recolor, rotation, effects; same clear space). Use them on white or light backgrounds.

Do not invent new sub-brand logos. If a product has no official logo, use the DIGI-TEXX logo and set the product name as text in Roboto Bold next to it (outside the clear space).
