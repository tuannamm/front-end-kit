# Brand voice and copy

## Audience

Business decision-makers in many industries (banking, insurance, logistics, healthcare, public sector, manufacturing, retail…) who look for **BPO and digital solutions**. They are busy, skeptical of ads, and want proof.

## Core voice

| Trait | Meaning | In practice |
|---|---|---|
| **Trustworthy & data-driven** | Every message is reliable and proven with real statistics | Use concrete numbers: years, volumes, accuracy %, SLA, languages, certifications (ISO 27001, ISO 9001…). Never invent figures — ask the user or leave a `[metric]` placeholder. |
| **Informative & efficient** | Every communication is packed with valuable information | Lead with the benefit, then the proof. Cut filler. |
| **Innovative & direct** | Clear messages powered with creativity | One idea per headline. Fresh verbs. No clichés. |

Supporting:
- **Tone:** professional, confident, informative, with a touch of thought leadership.
- **Language:** clear, concise, action-oriented.

Brand line: *"We are not business as usual, we accompany other businesses and develop sustainable growth. Keep your messages smart, direct, and practical."*

## The balance

| Too complicated ✗ | **Innovative & direct ✓** | Too "salesy" ✗ |
|---|---|---|
| Hard to memorize; academic; dense jargon | Business language, but no overly technical terms | Pushy ads; hype; desperate calls to action |

## Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Keep the headline simple and direct | Pack many messages into one headline/ad/post |
| Be innovative when giving valuable business details | Pour in a massive amount of academic knowledge |
| Use strong CTAs with a touch of creativity | Sound desperate for action ("CLICK NOW!", "Download file!!") |
| Build an audience-oriented communication journey | Be vague or leave the message incomplete |

## CTA upgrades (from → to)

| From (flat) | To (on-brand) |
|---|---|
| Get a free consultant | Let's get in touch |
| View our service now | Explore DIGI-TEXX services |
| Submit your CV | Be a part of DIGI-TEXX |
| Request a Demo | Discover our Demo |

## Writing rules

- Brand name: always **DIGI-TEXX** (all caps, hyphen). Products: **DIGI-XTRACT, DIGI-SCAN, DIGI-DMS, ONE DIGI-SOFT**. Website: **www.digi-texx.com**. Tagline: *advanced digitizing services* (lowercase).
- Headlines: ≤ 8 words where possible. Sentence case for body headings; display titles may be uppercase in the italic heading face.
- Structure: Hook (problem or outcome) → Proof (number, client type, certification) → CTA.
- Vietnamese copy: same voice; professional "chúng tôi / quý doanh nghiệp" register; avoid slang. Use Arial or Roboto (both support Vietnamese diacritics).
- Emoji: none in formal materials; at most 1–2 relevant ones in social posts.

## Example

✗ "DIGI-TEXX is a leading, world-class, best-in-class provider of comprehensive end-to-end AI-powered intelligent document processing solutions leveraging cutting-edge OCR/ICR/NLP/ML technologies! Contact us NOW!"

✓ "Turn paper backlogs into searchable data — at [99.5]% accuracy. DIGI-TEXX has digitized [X] million pages for banks and insurers since 2003. **Discover our Demo →**"
