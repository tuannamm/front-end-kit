# Branded applications

Patterns from the guideline's "Branded Merchandise" pages (see `assets/reference/guideline-sheet-3.jpg`, `-4.jpg`).

## Signage
Square logo tile mounted as a 3D box sign (navy face, white letters, blue dots) on the building facade.

## Business card (name card)
- Front: navy `#26385D`/`#1E1D23` field, horizontal white logo centered, dashed blue square-dot border strips (echo of the logo dots) top and bottom.
- Back: navy with white panel; QR code left; name in Roboto Bold (white or ink), title in Roboto Italic; contact lines with small blue icons (phone, email, web, address).

## Folder / brochure cover
White base, large diagonal blue panel (`#2582D7` → `#004EBE`) cutting from bottom-right, city photo inside a triangular crop, headline in white uppercase spaced Roboto (e.g. "DIGITAL SOLUTIONS PROVIDER"), horizontal logo top-right, website bottom.

## Letterhead / invoice / Word documents
- Header: square logo top-left, angled navy + blue stripe band across top, contact line (phone, email, web, address) with blue icons in Roboto/Arial 8–9 pt.
- Footer: angled blue/navy shape bottom-right; version / issue date / page number line in 7–8 pt.
- Body: Arial (Office default) or Roboto 10–11 pt, ink `#231F20`; headings bold, blue `#2582D7`.
- Tables: header blue fill white text; totals row blue.
- Vietnamese or shared Office docs: use **Arial** / **Arial Narrow** throughout.

## Bag and T-shirt
- Canvas tote: white/natural with illustrated slogans ("Transform for tomorrow", "Stay digitized, stay connected, stay with DIGI-TEXX") in navy/red/blue, logo below.
- T-shirt: DIGI-TEXX Blue shirt; front left-chest "WE" mark with "DIGITIZE / ANALYZE / OPTIMIZE" in amber/white; back small square logo at upper back.

## Event communication
Booths, backdrops, standees and LED screens: dark navy tech backgrounds with network/world-map graphics, isometric 3D tech illustrations, mascot robot, big white headline (e.g. "GLOBAL DIGITAL SOLUTIONS PROVIDER IN VIETNAM", "A German technology BPO company in Vietnam since 2003"), service icon row, logo prominent top. Amber/orange diagonal accents allowed beside blue.

## Social media communication
- Announcements (appointments, awards, events): blue gradient background, photo in a rounded/angled frame, white headline, logo top-left.
- Service carousels: dark blue frames, mascot, service name in white bold, icon rows.
- Recruitment: bright blue base, "WE ARE HIRING" in display type, photos of the team.
- Culture/events (anniversary, team building): may use amber/orange as a secondary field, collage of photos, still with DIGI-TEXX blue and logo.
- Always: one message per post, real numbers, creative CTA (see `voice.md`).
