# Imagery, banners, and social posts

## Characteristic patterns (backgrounds/decoration)

Tech motifs used as construction decoration and backgrounds — **not** the dominant element of a design:
- Glowing circuit-board traces on deep blue (`assets/patterns/pattern-circuit-left.jpg`, `pattern-circuit-right.jpg`).
- Network globe of connected dots and lines (`assets/patterns/pattern-network-globe.jpg`).
- Gears / service icons in a ring held by hands, the digital service metaphor (`assets/patterns/pattern-service-gears.jpg`).
- Shield + keyhole for data security (`assets/patterns/pattern-security-shield.jpg`).
- Blue wireframe wave mesh (`assets/patterns/pattern-wave-mesh.png`).
- Soft blue radial glow and floating bokeh dots (blue, with a rare amber dot).

Palette of patterns: deep navy `#0D1120`–`#1A489E` base, bright `#2582D7`/`#30AAE0` lines, white highlights.

## Featured photography

Used together to build a consistent identity:
- Real professionals at work: Asian (Vietnamese) staff in office settings, scanning documents, at monitors, team discussions — natural, confident, business-casual.
- Hands interacting with holographic UI / icons (security lock, gear ring, data).
- Document scanning and large-format scanners, server rooms.
- Data/AI overlays: OCR, deep learning, machine learning, NLP labels over screens.
- Big, hopeful landscapes (mountains, river valleys, wind farms, sky) for intro/closing — sustainability and growth.
- Architecture with blue sky / blue glass, city skylines (Ho Chi Minh City at dusk).

Treatment: cool, blue-dominant grade; high clarity; slight blue overlay (`#2582D7` at 20–40%, multiply or color) when an image sits behind text. Avoid warm/orange-heavy grades unless it is an event/culture post.

Avoid: cartoon clip-art, cheesy handshake stock, neon pink/purple cyberpunk, dark horror tones, messy desks, non-business settings, fake-looking AI faces, visible competitor brands.

## Image generation prompt recipe

Append to any prompt for a DIGI-TEXX visual:

> corporate technology aesthetic, deep navy and bright electric blue (#2582D7) color palette, glowing blue circuit lines and network nodes, clean modern composition, soft blue light glow, professional, high detail, cinematic lighting, negative space for text, no text, no logos

Variants:
- People: "Vietnamese office professionals working with document scanners and computers, bright modern office, blue holographic data overlay".
- Abstract hero: "3D glowing blue brain/microchip on dark circuit platform, bokeh, isometric".
- Security: "hand touching holographic blue shield with keyhole, data protection".
- Closing: "aerial wind farm by the sea, clear blue sky, bright daylight".

Always add the real logo afterwards as an overlay (never let an image model draw the logo or brand name).

## Banners and social posts

Layout patterns seen in brand applications:
- Dark navy/blue tech background + white headline in Roboto Bold or the italic display face + blue gradient pill CTA + logo top-left or top-right.
- Diagonal blue panels and stripes (folder/letterhead style): white canvas cut by angled `#2582D7`/navy shapes.
- Mascot robot as a friendly guide for event/booth/recruitment visuals.
- Recruitment ("We are hiring") and event posts may add amber `#F6B71E` or lime `#A5CB1F` accents — still with blue leading.

Specs:
| Format | Size (px) | Logo |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn / Facebook post | 1200 × 1200 or 1200 × 628 | Top-left, ≈ 18% width |
| LinkedIn banner | 1584 × 396 | Left, keep clear of profile photo area |
| Facebook cover | 1640 × 624 | Right side |
| Event backdrop / booth | any | Large, top center/left, clear space respected |
| Roll-up / standee | 850 × 2000 mm typical | Top |

Text on image: ≤ 20% of area; headline ≤ 8 words; always include www.digi-texx.com or a clear CTA.

## Image editing checklist

- Blue leads; no other dominant hue.
- Logo from `assets/logos`, correct variant, clear space, ≥ 120 px.
- Pattern backgrounds sit behind a solid/blurred zone where the logo and text go.
- Export: PNG for graphics with text, JPG (quality ≥ 85) for photos, sRGB.
